# Fall 2027 Robot + AI PhD Faculty Candidate List

Updated: August 6, 2026

## Scope and interpretation

This list uses the ten highest-ranked US universities in EduRank's March 15, 2026 robotics research-performance ranking:

1. Carnegie Mellon University
2. MIT
3. Stanford University
4. Georgia Tech
5. University of Southern California
6. UC Berkeley
7. University of Michigan
8. University of Pennsylvania
9. UIUC
10. University of Washington

The ranking is bibliometric, not a definitive ranking of PhD training quality or current lab strength. Faculty fit is more important than the ordering.

The applicant profile used for screening is:

- ALE co-first/core contributor: benchmark-scale agent execution, tool/computer-use environments, automated evaluators, RLVR, and frontier-model evaluation.
- Ongoing robot-learning data benchmark: EgoVerse/EgoDex/HOI4D-style egocentric manipulation and interaction data cleaning/curation.
- Robotics and perception: VLA integration, robot delivery, zero-shot UAV-to-UAV detection, optical-flow tracking under high-speed motion and strong ego-motion.
- Agent runtime and infrastructure: reproducible sandbox execution, MCP/tool interfaces, and scalable task environments.

`A` means a strong thematic match worth prioritizing. `B` means a credible but more complementary match. Being listed does not establish that a professor has Fall 2027 funding or will admit a student this cycle.

## Highest-priority leads

These leads have unusually direct overlap with the applicant's experience or an explicit recruiting signal.

1. **Ankit Goyal - Michigan Robotics/CSE, incoming Fall 2027.** Explicitly looking for students. Works on robot foundation models, dexterous manipulation, 3D perception, VLA, and simulation benchmarking. `RoboLab` directly overlaps with scalable task generation, benchmark execution, and generalization evaluation.
2. **Daniel Seita - USC CS/SLURM.** Lab explicitly states it is actively recruiting students and that PhD students receive funding. Works on robot manipulation, foundation models, data augmentation, and `ManipBench`.
3. **Yue Wang - USC CS.** Recent USC project with Daniel Seita explicitly studies a benchmark comparing methods that co-train on action-free human video and robot-demonstration data. This is an unusually direct match to EgoVerse/EgoDex/HOI4D-style work.
4. **Gokul Swamy - UIUC, incoming Fall 2027.** Publicly states that he will recruit PhD students. Works on interactive decision-making, imitation learning, RL/RLHF, robust agents, language models, and robotic manipulation.
5. **Yaqi Xie - UIUC ECE/CS affiliate.** Publicly recruiting. Works on neuro-symbolic/structured reasoning, foundation models, spatial reasoning, trustworthy agents, and robotics.
6. **Saurabh Gupta - UIUC ECE.** Works on generalizable embodied agents, hand-object interaction, robot learning from videos, imitation learning, mobile manipulation, and humanoids.
7. **Jiayuan Mao - Penn CIS/GRASP, incoming Fall 2026.** Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics background; neuro-symbolic concepts, continual learning, reasoning, and planning in the physical world.
8. **Dinesh Jayaraman - Penn CIS/GRASP.** Vision-based robot learning, generalist policies, long-horizon manipulation, and coding-agent-driven robot learning. Strong bridge from agent systems to embodied learning.
9. **Roberto Martin-Martin - UT Austin CS (additional school).** RobIn lab explicitly says it is looking for new members; robot learning, offline human demonstrations, mobile manipulation, and human environments.
10. **Chenfeng Xu - UT Austin CS (additional school).** Recent Berkeley collaborator with work on cross-embodiment and zero-shot robot policy transfer, including collaboration with Masayoshi Tomizuka and Ken Goldberg.
11. **Manling Li - Northwestern CS (additional school).** RAGEN/VAGEN, multi-turn agent RL, EmbodiedBench, automated/code-based rewards, and embodied reasoning. Very strong ALE/RLVR match. Her lab page cautions that individual outreach emails may not receive a response.
12. **Sayash Kapoor - Berkeley School of Information, incoming Fall 2027.** Explicitly recruiting for frontier-AI evaluation. Not a robotics PI, but an exceptionally direct match for ALE/open-world and long-horizon agent evaluation.

## Recommended application strategy

Do not cold-email all 108 names. Use three queues:

1. **Immediate outreach (roughly 15-25 PIs):** new PIs, explicit recruiting pages, direct ALE/RLVR or robot-data overlap, and professors with existing Berkeley/ALE/Tomizuka connections.
2. **Formal-application targets:** established labs at CMU, MIT, Stanford, Berkeley, Georgia Tech, Penn, Michigan, UIUC and UW. These may not respond to cold email, but should be named in applications where appropriate.
3. **Warm-introduction targets:** use ALE/RDI/BAIR, Dawn Song, Zhuo Xu, Masayoshi Tomizuka, and existing frontier-lab contacts to identify collaborators or mutual connections.

The strongest concise research pitch is:

> I build benchmark-scale agent environments and automated evaluators that support RLVR, and I am now applying this experience to robot-learning data and embodied-agent evaluation, with additional hands-on experience in VLA systems and high-speed UAV perception.
